# FIFA 20 Team of the Week 1 (TOTW 1)

[![Image 3: FIFA 20 Ultimate Team - Team of the Week 1](http://www.fifplay.com/img/public/fifa-20-team-of-the-week-01.jpg)](http://www.fifplay.com/fifa-20/players/?squad=totw-1 "FIFA 20 TOTW 1")
##### FIFA 20 Team of the Week 1 - September 18

[FIFA 20 TOTW](http://www.fifplay.com/fifa-20-totw/) 1 In-form players (IFs) are available from September 18 (6pm UK) until September 25, 2019 (6pm UK) in the [packs](http://www.fifplay.com/fifa-20/packs/).

#### Starting 11

*   GK: **Manuel Neuer** (89)
*   LB: **Nicolás Tagliafico** (84)
*   CB: **Laurent Koscielny** (84)
*   RB: **Serge Aurier** (82)
*   CDM: **Thomas Delaney** (84)
*   CM: **Frenkie de Jong** (86)
*   RM: **Nani** (83)
*   RM: **Moreno** (84)
*   ST: **Sadio Mané** (89)
*   ST: **Karim Benzema** (88)
*   ST: **Dries Mertens** (88)

#### Substitutes and Reserves

*   RW: **Mustapha Bundu** (75)

[Team of the Week](http://www.fifplay.com/encyclopedia/team-of-the-week/) is a squad in FUT, which is collected by EA and is formed by the best players from around the world who were in-form and have performed very well in their past week's international and/or club competitions.
